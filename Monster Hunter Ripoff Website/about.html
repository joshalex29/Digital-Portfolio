Monster Hunter is an action role-playing game (RPG) series where players take on the role of a hunter to hunt small and large monsters in quests and expeditions. Players can then create a large variety of unique weapons and armor sets from monster parts, obtained by slaying or capturing monsters. The more the players progress, the higher rank their hunter will be. The hunter will face much more rarer and vicious monsters. But in turn, they will have a chance to create the finest quality weapons and armor pieces to adapt with the strength of the monsters and become the master of the hunt.

The Hunter's Guild is the central governing association of all Monster Hunters. Its main purpose is to prevent the ecosystem from being further damaged, in an attempt to prevent extant species from becoming extinct. It intends to coordinate humans (and other sapient species) with nature, despite the two having became separated over millennia. To enforce this policy, the Guild uses trained experts called hunters. The populations of monsters in each region is recorded, and hunters are not allowed to hunt repeatedly in the same species unless otherwise stated. Poaching can result in a multitude of punishments, but the most common is death at the hands of a Guild Knight.
